Anvi insight

Both cost measures are favourable, and on their own both are misleading. Earned value stands at $126.0M against a planned value of $153.6M for the elapsed period — an SPI of 0.82. The program converts plan into delivered value 18% slower than scheduled.

CPI of 1.06 says the work that has been done cost less than budgeted. That is real efficiency, and it is why cost variance reads green. But the $1.7M under-run is not primarily efficiency — 40 of 92 initiatives have not started, and unstarted work does not spend. The favourable cost position is substantially a by-product of the schedule position.

Recommendation: report SPI alongside cost variance. A program at CPI 1.06 / SPI 0.82 is not an on-budget program — it is a late program that has not yet paid for being late.
